Start with work on self, then take it to the world.

Elkdonis Arts Paratheatre Labs are perceptual artist and scientist seers using art and theatre as their primary investigative tool. The work is self exploratory, reductionist ritual and theatre.

Jason teaches inside that circle. Rock balancing is where he starts, and the labs are where it leads.

How to work with him

  1. Write a short note

    Say who you are and what draws you to the work. Two lines are enough.

  2. Sit in on a gathering

    Come to a standing gathering and see how the circle holds itself.

  3. Commit to the circle

    The labs ask for regular meetings and kept commitments, so say so honestly.

  4. Take it outward

    After work on self comes work on the world, at the pace the circle can carry.
