Artist, rock balancer and practitioner

Jason Ford, aka sunjye

A living archive of teachings, practices and creative explorations. Jason balances stone and builds ritual theatre on the same principle: find the point where weight and attention agree, then stay still enough to let it hold.

The work starts with the self and moves outward to the world. It is reductionist ritual and theatre, practised in small online circles, in the tradition of Antero Alli, Jerzy Grotowski, G. I. Gurdjieff, E. J. Gold and Carlos Castaneda.

Para Theater is presented by Elkdonis Arts Collective, formed in Newmarket, Ontario in 1990 and now working between Paris, Los Angeles and Toronto. The work is meant to be experienced, not sold.
