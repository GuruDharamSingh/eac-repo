The artist

Photo spot: sunjye balancing stone

Jason Ford, aka sunjye.

Artist, rock balancer and practitioner. A living archive of teachings, practices and creative explorations.

The work is self exploratory and reductionist: rituals and theatre that start with work on self and move outward to world work. Circles meet online, and everyone in them keeps the same few commitments, in the tradition of Antero Alli, Jerzy Grotowski, G. I. Gurdjieff, E. J. Gold and Carlos Castaneda.

You don't force attention, you invoke it.
