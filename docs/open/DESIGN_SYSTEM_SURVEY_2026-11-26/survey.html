1 · The model

One object at several sizes

A face is the card in a grid. A surface is the popup it opens, drawn by one frame (masthead, body with an optional rail, foot). Surfaces stack inside the one dialog. A stage docks the same descriptors into the page, and a page is the frame in the page flow. The detail view is whatever a descriptor renders into the frame.

Detail views are not one component. A thread renders through threadViewParts() (returns main, rail and kicker; ThreadView.tsx:50); content renders through ContentDetail with three layouts (hub popup at size full, SurfacePage, side sheet); pictures open in SimpleLightbox (cms-ui gallery) or PieceLightbox (content, a div role="dialog" over its parent, not the native dialog).

Naming problems found.
  • --eac-accent is the generic fallback in surface, blocks and primitives, gold in innergathering, brass in fourthway, and a role in the dev test theme; the legacy eac-theme.css defines its own --eac-ink and a translucent --eac-panel.
  • Two spellings for text on the accent: --eac-accent-on (gradients ramps, read by forms.css) and --eac-on-accent (dev test theme, theme-language.md); plus --sf-on-accent, --pr-accent-on, --blk-on-accent.
  • shadcn --accent is a hover wash in most apps but the brand gold in ifac and innergathering.
  • forms.css re-declares 17 --sf-* on .frm-pv-sheet by hand (forms.css:1623-1647) and can drift from surface.css.
  • "Look" means three things: hidden-enneagram presets, katie card looks, and site-forms look="site".

5 · Kinds

What a face is about sets its glyph, size and accent

Every --sf-kind-<k> defaults to the site accent unless the app sets --eac-kind-<k>. The swatches use elktours' dark-theme accents from the uploaded specimen; place (#8fb3c4) is the only kind with its own default. define has no [data-kind] rule (what it inherits is unconfirmed).

6 · Looks across the network

Each site's palette, laid out by role

Seven core roles per site (ground, card, ink, muted, line, accent, danger), then the extras each site adds. Most sites already use these seven plus one to four extras. ≈ marks hex converted from an HSL triplet. The .dark blocks in amrit-canada, arts-collective, elktours and innergathering are probably never applied: no class toggle was found (unconfirmed).

Sites

Package looks and design directions

The owner's stated preferences (memory notes, 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-20): Studio light (paper ground, white surfaces, one blue accent, Inter + Source Serif 4, 6–10px radii, shadows only on popovers). Forms: ledger rows, fields that read as fields, square corners, blue for selection, gold for Publish; in the dark popup, the grey-gold "A" palette over the blue-grey Studio dark. Rejected: broadsheet, cream-and-gold "parchment" forms, bracket chips, helper microcopy. elktours: Basteleur Moonlight for text, Basteleur Bold for titles, Venture for labels, Brothers kept only in the header brand (L–K joined).

"A theme from five colours" (THEME_SYSTEM_2026-11-22)proposal + sandbox pageFive layers: palette (5 stored colours) → shades (9 per colour, derived) → roles (pointers such as "ink = colour 2, shade 900", never values) → tones per section (plain, soft, deep, accent) → blocks read roles. First sites: elkdonis-arts.org (Bon elements) and Amrit Canada.

Decided by the owner

12 core roles; dark mode optional; one shared bronze (#c8a45b, elktours' brass, now also sunjay's); kind colours follow the site accent for now; the theme is "header, footer, menu, title, subtitle and body text, plus perhaps a triad of accent colours".

Not decided

Which proposal is canonical; presets ("not convinced"); five slots or another number (N1); Amrit's fifth colour (N2); ground on elkdonis-arts.org (N3); accent-trio order (N4); tone names (N5); six text styles (N6); off-palette colours in blocks (N7); dark mode later or never (N8); the --eac- prefix collision.

How today's palettes fold into seven slots

An illustration, not a decision: the five-colour model's slots (ground, ink, accent, accent 2, accent 3), plus a danger red and one optional extra for sites that carry a fourth signal colour. Panel, muted, line and tints come from shades of these, as in the five-colour proposal. Values are each site's current ones from section 6; the first two rows are the proposal's own starting palettes.

  • Five seeds cover every site above once panel, muted and line are derived; the sixth (danger) is already a fixed red in the five-colour sandbox for Amrit (#b3261e) and Fire 800 for elkdonis-arts.org.
  • The seventh slot is needed by hidden-enneagram (four centre colours) and, if kinds get their own colours, by any site with a place or route colour. Otherwise kinds read the accent trio.
  • Contrast to watch: in the sandbox, on-accent text on Fire measures 4.33:1 and on Amrit's burnt orange 4.41:1, both just under 4.5:1 for body text.

9 · Open items

Gaps this survey turned up

  • One card system. SurfaceCard, HubLinkCard and defineHubCard all draw hub tiles. The cornerstone picked defineHubCard for hubs; its chrome does not yet match SurfaceCard + SurfaceFrame.
  • One role vocabulary. Four overlapping proposals (Frame, theme language, five colours, dev test theme) and one built mechanism (Site style). None is read by surface.css yet.
  • Wizard pieces in cms-ui still use Tailwind; the 2a step strip and the edit-in-place, sentence and registry field treatments exist only as designs.
  • Light theme for elktours is drawn (bleu de Paris, white, yellow) but not mapped; the surface primary-fill tokens it needs are in place.
  • Dormant dark blocks in four apps and a content.css dark set that loses to --sf-* on hosts that load surface.css.
  • Reference docs (frontend-ui.md, packages.md) are about two months behind the code: counts, Mantine apps, new packages.
  • Do not rebuild without asking: the /center bento console, the hub GalleryFace drop well, the ProfileGallery edit bar, and header-led IFAC Files face (all reverted).
