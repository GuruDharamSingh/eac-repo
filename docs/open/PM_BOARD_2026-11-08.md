## North star
The similar apps (sunjay, katie-the-potter, hidden-enneagram, danamccool, elktours,
amrit-canada) collapse toward **one app fed from a central source** ("center
factory"). This is long-term work that lands piece by piece. The central services now are the
**LMS+MCP** (action registry, onboarding, assistant), the **Channel** (promo and production
planning), the **Studio** (the creation CMS) and the **Hub**, which /manage folds into.
