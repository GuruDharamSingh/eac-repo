## His words, sorted

**1. Module view and sheet share uses.**
- A step can be seen, selected and answered in the sheet OR in the module: reflections, quizzes.
- The sheet stays open while the module is in theater mode or showing a document, so completion can continue beside it.

**2. Talk room.**
- A setting to produce a Talk room; its live chat is served into the sheet.
- The same Talk can be the video-conference link, or a Zoom link can be pasted in workshop settings, for wherever a "video call" button is needed.

**3. Journal visibility, corrected.**
- "By public I meant the cohort can view each other's. Not those who are not in the cohort."
- So there is no outside-public option. The choice is Private (each person and the guide) or shared within the group (Concurrent).

**4. The journal as a workplace.**
- Pinned material.
- A natural interval of days or weeks, set in the editor's settings.
- Either one continuous feed, or separated by the interval: each day or week lists the task or material to follow.
- A specific journal as well as the general journal.
- An option to be notified when someone posts.

**5. The journal can take over the module screen.**
- A type of step, or a session type, that is its own section: in settings, choose a session and change its type from the default to a "Cohort Space". It is a pinned, concurrent space with weekly or daily pinned material.
- A workshop can be a single section defined by one such step, even with week or day intervals.
- Otherwise the journal lives in the surface (frame / sheet).

**6. "Keep this grounded."** Re-review these in light of good practices, and of what the LMS package has already set out.

**7. Then stand it up at `elkdonis-arts.org/workspace`** (innergathering, org `inner_group`).
- A page listing the created workshops/spaces to enter, or create a new one.
- Hooked to a real org with the platform's packages: the content picker for adding material, the PDF viewer as on /books, profile settings, and possibly the LMS wiring.

## Vocabulary so far
- workshop = course; session = module; step = a kind of activity in a module.
- sheet: the steps and the concurrent space (presence, messages, timer, deadlines).
- surface frame: the Journal view, and a step opened large.
- "concurrent" (not cohort, not circle) for shared journals. He did say "Cohort Space" for the session type: confirm the name with him.

**8. Shared journal view (added later the same day).** In the shared (concurrent) journal view he wants BOTH an aggregated feed of everyone's entries AND a filter by participant. This applies to non-hosts too, when journals are shared: a participant sees the same two ways of looking.
