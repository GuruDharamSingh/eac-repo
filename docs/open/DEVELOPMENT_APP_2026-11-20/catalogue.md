## 3. Surprises
- `--eac-block-band` exists in 7 apps with 7 different values (gradients, `var(--ink)`, `hsl(var(--card))`, hex): same name, same job, no shared default.
- `--primary` is declared in 13 apps with 13 values. elktours `40 50% 57%` and sunjay `40 43% 63%` are near-identical bronzes, hand-copied. Same shadcn names, so name unification is trivial; value unification is a design decision.
- One hex under many names: danamccool `--color-foreground`, `--color-primary-foreground`, `--color-base-content` all `#060606`; `--color-primary`, `--color-accent`, `--color-info`, `--cyan` all `#48d0ed` (the palette fans one role across the Tailwind kit). elktours `--sf-fg`, `--tpi-fg`, `--tsp-ink`, `--ts-ink` all `#14284b`, and `--sf-accent`, `--tpi-k`, `--tsp-kl` all `#2350a0`: four stylesheets, one palette, four prefixes.
- elktours has 382 variables across 9 files and about 15 prefixes; innergathering has 140 `--ig-*` alone. These are the two heavy systems.
- Value formats differ: Site style `hsl` apps use bare triplets (`40 50% 57%`), ifac and danamccool use hex, `--blk-*` takes any colour. A naive "set role X everywhere" has to convert.
- ifac keeps a site theme, a per-person theme and a hub skin in three places; artdirect's person colour shares no editor with the others; forum reader prefs are cookies.
- 77 of 184 blocks cannot render outside their app, including all elktours Puck configs and 11 of 17 hidden-enneagram blocks. Only 40 are truly standalone.
- The catalogue throws on duplicate ids, so duplication shows up as re-declaration under a prefix (`dm-gallery-grid`), not as clashes.
