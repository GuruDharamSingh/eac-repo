About IFAC

A group of folks who joined together to provide currency in the art world.

Currency, you say? Let's define currency as directions of flow. This reflects our personal histories as artists, collectors, and dealers of beauty, commonly referred to as 'art.' Our goal is to help artists and art dealers show their art to the world.

— Eric Brummel

New York · Paris · Vancouver · San Francisco · Buenos Aires
