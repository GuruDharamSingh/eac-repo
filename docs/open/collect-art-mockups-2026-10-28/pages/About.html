About

What Collect-Art is, and the groups behind it.

Collect-Art.com

Collect-Art is a central site for buying and bidding on art from across the Arts-Collective network, proposed for the domain collect-art.com. It has two parts.

The auction series are occasional, curated auctions with a set run: lots are added over several weeks of silent bidding, and the series closes with a live online auction. The first series is presented by International Fine Arts Collectors.

The Market is always open. Any artist on the network can list work there and sell it outright, without waiting for an auction; some pieces also run an auction of their own.

I.F.A.C.

International Fine Arts Collectors is an online gallery and artist community representing independent fine artists and art dealers. It presents the first Collect-Art auction series and selects the work offered in it.

Arts-Collective Platform

The Arts-Collective Platform is the shared home for a network of arts organisations' sites, with arts-collective.com as its hub. The sites share one sign-in, so a collector who signs in here is signed in across the network.

Artists keep one profile on the platform and can sell, show and run auctions from it. The platform is run by Elkdonis Arts Collective.

Elkdonis

The name Elkdonis derives from the ancient Greek word Helkdonis, which carries the meaning of objective prayer: prayer not for the self, but outward, selfless, directed toward something greater. That orientation underlies everything the collective does.

Elkdonis Arts Collective

Jason Ford and Dana McCool founded the Elkdonis Arts Collective as a duo of artists working at the edge of experimentation: minimising habitual elaboration so that the viewer's stored expectations are confronted and, for a moment, set aside, allowing direct perception of oneself in the moment.

The collective has since grown into an international network with key members in Toronto, Los Angeles and Paris, working across performance art, creative writing, music, philosophy, digital infrastructure and marketing. It is a not-for-profit, runs the Arts-Collective Platform, and hosts the Collect-Art auction series.
