Several series on IFAC: mockups

Static mockups, 2026-10-18, for running more than one series on ifacgroup.com: a new /series page that introduces every series, a reworked page for one series, and where the series card and the page's intro copy are edited. Built with the live site's colours and type; real data for the current series, a placeholder second series ("Looking Closely", Thursdays, every other week). No app or package code was changed.

A · recommended for the index

Programme

Intro copy, then one wide general card per series (cover, description, schedule, hosts, its next session as one line), then every series' sessions in one dated list, each row marked with its series' colour, with a filter.

  • Reads top-down as "what IFAC offers, then when". Works for one, two or five series.
  • Cards are tall; with four or more series the list is far down.

B

Cards and calendar

Intro beside a short statement; series as portrait cards side by side; then a month calendar with each session as a coloured chip (a list on phones).

  • Compact; the calendar shows how the series interleave.
  • A calendar with few sessions looks empty; "I'm coming" moves to the session page.

C

Featured and lanes

One featured series large, with its video; the others smaller beside it; then one column of upcoming sessions per series.

  • Keeps a clear lead (the same series the landing slide leads with).
  • Lanes get narrow past two series; a session's date order across series is harder to see.

One series page, reworked

/series/<slug> led by the series, not this week: title, cover video, then the general card (About, schedule, hosts, Subscribe, sign-in). The next session is one band; the other sessions are rows that open their own pages. This week's write-up is no longer shown twice.

E · editing

The card and the page, in the hub

The planner's Series settings gain a "Card" part (colour, rhythm line, status chip, where it shows) with a live preview of the card and of the landing slide. A new organisation setting, "Series page", holds the intro heading and text and the order of the series. New series in /hub/compose get a Card step.
