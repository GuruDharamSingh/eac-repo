Assumptions to check
  1. Template pages (and their copy slots) come from each app. Owners can’t create new ones; new pages are always editor pages.
  2. The theme itself is changed in the existing theme editor. This card shows what the site uses and which pages are exceptions; exceptions change colours only.
  3. Profile replaces the org card form on /center. It describes the org to the network and doesn’t switch hub cards on or off.
  4. Sites of four pages or fewer open in the list with rows expanded; bigger sites open in the grid.
  5. A nested page sits under its parent’s address and is listed under it in Pages. A saved template is a snapshot: pages made from it don’t change when it does.
