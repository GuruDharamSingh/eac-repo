1Member Management hub card: collapsed card, pop-up, detail page

Members

One hub card replaces the People, Access, Artists & dealers, Visitors and Cloud storage tabs on every app’s /manage page. Every frame is live: click the card to open its pop-up, change a role, list someone, welcome an arrival, switch the visitor range.

Assumptions to check
  1. Storage access follows role: owner full, guide edit, member view, viewer none. “Syncing” is a queued role change in flight.
  2. Guides see people and arrivals and can welcome arrivals as viewers. Giving roles, listing, removing, storage and visitors stay with owners.
  3. Owners can make someone an owner, behind a confirm, because nobody can change an owner’s role afterwards.
  4. Orgs of five or fewer get one page instead of tabs, and the card lists the people by name.
  5. Arrivals queue oldest first, so the longest wait drives the “needs attention” line.
