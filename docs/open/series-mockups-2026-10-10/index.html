Series composer and series page: mockups

Static mockups, 2026-10-10, for composing a recurring meeting ("series") in the IFAC hub and for the public series page. Example series: Explorations in Art Forms by Non Professionals, IFAC, Mondays 6:00 PM Pacific. Names, titles, links and passcodes are placeholders. No app or package code was changed.

01 · hub

Compose page: One meeting or Series

Breadcrumb header (Hub › Compose › Meeting) in place of the title block. The Meeting/Event switch becomes "One meeting | Series" under a "Recurring series?" prompt, with two lines on what a series is. The one-meeting form below uses white fields on a tinted well. Choosing Series in the file swaps the form for a pointer to 02 or 03.

Screenshots: desktopphone

02 · hub · variant A

Series composer: series card and session timeline

A wide series card (cover, preview of the promotional card, title, description, cadence, first and last session, join URL, meeting ID, passcode), then a row of session squares joined by a line, with an "Add session" square. The chosen square opens its editor below the row: host, co-host, title, write-up, cover image, materials. The squares work in the file.

Effort
Higher. New layout component (squares, connector, editor placement, previous/next) plus the series-card form. The week editor's fields can reuse the planner's week form.
Planner mapping
Same per-week fields as SeriesPlannerSurface; the list becomes a row. Weeks are computed from the recurrence today (meeting-series.ts), so "Add session" would extend the last-session date rather than insert a free-standing session.
Phones
Under 720 px the row turns into a vertical timeline and the editor opens in place under the chosen session. Needs a separate layout and testing at two breakpoints.
Open
More than five or six sessions: scroll the row sideways inside the timeline, or show a window around the next session.

Screenshots: desktopphone

03 · hub · variant B

Series planner, extended

The existing planner with a collapsible "Series settings" card above the weeks (summary line when closed; cover, title, description, cadence, dates and meeting link when open), the vertical list of weeks unchanged in structure, one week open, and an "Add a week" row.

Effort
Lower. One new card inside SeriesPlannerSurface, an "Add a week" control, and the field contrast change.
Planner mapping
Direct: the weeks are the current WeekForm rows; breadcrumb and page mount stay as in SeriesPlannerPage.tsx.
Phones
Already a vertical list; only the week header and the add-link row restack.
Trade-off
Less visual sense of the series as a sequence; the settings card adds length above the weeks while open.

Screenshots: desktopphone

04 · public site

Series page with the weekly email band

Dark .ifs series head with the next-session card, the "Coming up" list with session 2 open (write-up, cover, materials), and beside it a "Keep up to date with this series" band: email and optional name, one email the day before each session with host, title and Zoom link, and a sample of the email. Rows carry scroll-margin-top so a jump to #week-2 leaves the card fully below the sticky header.

Phones
Under 1000 px the band comes before the list; the header shortens to 56 px and the scroll margin follows it.
Open
A per-series email subscription is not an existing feature as far as these mockups checked (unknown); it needs storage, a sender, and an unsubscribe link.

Screenshots: desktopphone · after a jump to #week-2: desktopphone

Common to all four

  • Hub palette from apps/ifac/src/app/globals.css (.ifac-hub-scope); public palette from apps/ifac/src/app/site.css (revision 4).
  • Field contrast: white fields, 1.5 px #7d705a edge (4.8:1 against the field), on a #f3eee4 well; placeholders #6b6259 (6.0:1). Text pairs are listed in each file's CSS header.
  • Checked at 1280 px and 360 px in headless Chrome 124: no horizontal scroll; an automated pass found no text pair under 4.5:1 (solid grounds only; text over the cover gradients was checked by hand).
