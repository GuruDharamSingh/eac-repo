Weekly email

Keep up to date with this series

One email a week, the day before each session: who is hosting, the title, what to look at, and the Zoom link.

Each email has a link to stop. The address is used for this series only. Signed-in members can turn it on from their account.

What the email looks like
Subject: Tomorrow, 6:00 PM Pacific: Session title Host: Host name Join: https://zoom.example/j/0000000000 Meeting ID: 000 0000 0000 · Passcode: PASSCODE Materials and the write-up follow, then a link to stop.
Mockup note.
  • The email band sits beside "Coming up" on wide screens and stays in view while the list scrolls; under 1000 px it comes before the list.
  • Cut-off card: session-list.tsx gives each row id="week-N", and site.css has no scroll-margin-top, so a jump to #week-2 puts the card top under the 64 px sticky header. Here each row has scroll-margin-top: calc(header height + 24px), which also follows the shorter phone header. Open #week-2 to check.
  • The linked week should also open on arrival; today only the next week opens by default.
