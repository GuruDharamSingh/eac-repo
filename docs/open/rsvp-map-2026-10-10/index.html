Inconsistencies and risks (most consequential first)

  1. risk Authorization hole in a running legacy app. apps/inner-gathering/.../meetings/[id]/attendees/route.ts:66–99 lets any signed-in user set any user's status on any thread. Verified in code; live probe not run.
  2. risk The legacy reminder tick is network-wide. apps/inner-gathering/src/lib/reminders.ts:28 mails yes rows in every org except amrit, overrides sunjay's "off", and will reach series subscribers.
  3. dup Three flavour→status maps. In RSVP_FLAVOURS, cms-ui DEFAULT_FLAVOURS and the innergathering relabel, the same answer means different statuses. The IFAC hub face offers answers that the server rejects.
  4. in progress Two meanings of yes. A series subscription and attendance share thread_rsvps. center, calendar, forum, alerts and the address book will count subscribers as attendees.
  5. inconsistent Cycle expiry is applied in 4 route GETs only. Stale yes rows fill capacity, light, counts and reminders. Answering again in a new cycle sends no confirmation.
  6. dup Five writers of one row (setRsvpStatus, setMeetingAttendance, confirmOrderPaid, series subscription, legacy raw INSERTs). Six copies of route A with four behaviours; guest route E in four apps.
  7. Capacity and waitlist are partial. Guest routes, confirmOrderPaid and createThreadOrder skip the limit. Nothing writes or promotes from waitlist.
  8. Paid seats. The price check exists only in innergathering and arts-collective. A DELETE cancels a paid seat without touching the order.
  9. in progress Series week topics have no unique key (week 1 has two threads). The first RSVPer becomes the author. A "no" creates a topic.
  10. Sign-in loses the intent. Most RSVP prompts go to bare /login, and none carries an RSVP intent.
