Practice routine

The same few steps every day, a weekly rhythm, and an ending. Suits a daily practice entry or any routine: reading, drawing, study. Steps stay inside the circle unless its owner turns public outlets on. It branches at the start (quiet or recorded) and at the end (repeat, a series, or rest).
