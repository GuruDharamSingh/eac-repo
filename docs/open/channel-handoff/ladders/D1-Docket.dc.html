What each control changes

  • WhenMoves the step to a day. A day with a routine slot for the outlet shows “slot” and fills it; a slot that is taken warns instead of stacking.
  • TimeThe slot's time is marked. Another time keeps the step off the slot; dated steps of an event keep their day and only the time moves.
  • WhereOutlets and the identity it goes out as. An outlet that is not connected is shown but cannot be chosen.
  • How importantThree levels: can wait, normal, matters. It sets the order on Home and in the dock and whether one reminder mail goes out.
  • The ideaThe note the step started from, editable. It travels with the step into the tool that opens (here the clip editor).
  • Skip or neverSkip drops the step for this piece only. Never asks for which pieces (this kind, or any) and records a rule that can be undone in settings.
