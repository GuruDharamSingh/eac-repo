Two days, four presses and about a kilo of soy ink. We set out to learn whether the lab could give up solvent clean-up for good.

The short answer is yes, with two catches. Soy inks stay open longer on the slab, which is lovely for monotype and a nuisance for anything that needs a crisp second colour.

Nobody missed the smell of white spirit.
