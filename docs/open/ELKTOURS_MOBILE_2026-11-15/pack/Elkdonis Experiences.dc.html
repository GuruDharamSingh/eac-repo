Turn 6 · Feed & forum on Discover

Three rows at the foot of Discover

The feed, the places catalogue and the forum are now rows in the catalogue's style. The feed row is the largest and previews the newest post; the forum row is the thinnest, at the bottom. The feed row opens a Feed page, and each post opens as a preview with comments.

How it works Link posts load the site inside the preview, with a full-screen view. Many news sites refuse to be shown inside another page, so lemonde.fr and theartnewspaper.com may stay blank here; the real app would fall back to the browser for those. Every feed post is also a public topic in a new Feed section of the forum. Comments in the preview and replies in the forum are the same list (6d, 6e). Forum link cards and place websites open in the same in-app view.

Turn 5 · Elkdonis Forum, Local Artists, tags

Paris, with Local Artists

Discover now has a forum: sections with sub-sections, topics, replies, and a choice of who can read each topic. Guests, guides, artists and the office all post in it. Experiences link to an About page and a Local Artists page, and places carry tags that the catalogue filters by.

From packages/cms-ui · ForumSurface, apps/sunjay The forum follows the surface's shape. The Discover tile shows the forum at tile size: section names, "N new" and the latest titles. Guests never see it; it shows on the guide phones (5g). Replies and new topics are shared across the phones.

Turn 4 · Discover, places & the feed

A home that the guides keep

The bottom bar is now Discover, Tours and Experiences. Discover keeps the week's walk, then the guides' favourite places, the experiences coming, the guides themselves and a feed of what they share. A catalogue of every place sits at the bottom.

How it works A place opens as a sheet with its address, hours, links out and the tour it sits on. Businesses show a note that their own profile is coming later. Guides add places and star the ones they like. Starred places appear under Favourite places on Discover. Try starring one on 4i. Guides share a post or a link from their board. The office confirms it on 4k, and then it appears in the feed on Discover. Stops on a tour now open in place: what you see there, a Street View still, the place card and the 3D map. The places, posts and links are placeholders.

Turn 3 · the tour page

What a tour opens with

The tour page now opens on an arched window. It can hold the fly-over film, the live 3D route or the photographs. A tour only shows what its guide has switched on, and guests can switch between them with the pills under the window.

From apps/elktours · tour page & assets On the site, a tour page opens on its film if it has one. Without a film it opens on the 3D route preview, and without that on the cover picture. Here the guide can also choose which one comes first. Films are always shown whole because the map credit is burned into the edges. On the phone the film sits in a 16:9 frame inside the arch, so the arch never crops it. As on the site, the 3D map only loads when a guest presses play. Tapping a stop under The route flies the map to that stop. The switches are shared with the guest phones. Turn the film off on 3f and 3a opens on the 3D route instead. The map, the film and the photos are stand-ins. The real ones come from each tour's assets.

Turn 2 · artists & experiences

Something strange on the way

An experience is not a tour. A local artist or resident hosts it, usually at one stop of a guide's tour, and sometimes on its own by request. Artists sign up, post what they offer and when they're free. Guides put experiences on their stops, and the artist confirms each date. All of this happens on a shared board.

From the experiences spec · assumptions Experiences get their own colour: verdigris, the patina on old copper. They also get their own shape, round portholes instead of the arched frames used for tours. An experience is a thread of kind experience, with the artist in experience_people. It sits on a stop through tour_stops.experience_id. Each date invitation is a service_commissions row: asked, confirmed or declined. On its own, an experience is enquiry-only for now, the spec's default. The office reviews each new one and publishes it as Public or Members only. The phones share data with turn 1. Accept an invitation on 2e and it shows up on 2g. Publish on 2h and it appears on Discover.

Elkdonis Experiences · mobile · direction 1

Scheduling for guides, a travel guide for guests

One app, two sides. Guides and the office set up slots: dates, start times, places from minimum to maximum, when booking closes, waitlist and price. Guests browse Paris as an art-magazine city guide and book those slots. Every phone below is live, and they share one set of data: book a place on a guest phone and it appears on the guide phones.

Built from apps/elktours · assumptions Palette and type are the site's own "zinc and brass": Basteleur, Brothers, Venture. Medieval cues: arched picture frames, chamfered buttons, double rules, Roman numerals. The layout follows your reference: dark cards, pill chips, floating nav. A slot is one dated departure (tour_departures). Minimum, maximum, booking cutoff, waitlist and price sit on each slot, with defaults taken from the experience. Dana McCool is the guide. "Admin" is the company office, which sees every guide and assigns slots. Margaux, the prices and the photos are placeholders. Below the minimum at cutoff, the guide decides. The Tweaks panel can switch this to auto-cancel, and can move the clock forward.
