About

Elkdonis Experiences

A guide to Paris made by the people who show it: painters, printmakers and other artists who live here and take small groups through the parts of the city they know best.

I · Tours

A guide leads each tour on set dates, for a small group. You book a place on one of those dates, and the guide meets you at the start.

II · Experiences

An experience is something a local artist does with you: a performance, a portrait, a recording, a thing you make together. Most wait at a stop on a tour; some open on their own, by request.

III · Places & the forum

The guides keep a list of the places they like, and a forum where guests, guides and artists talk about the city.
