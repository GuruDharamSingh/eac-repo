# Confirm wording, wave 03 (dock forms, wiring wave 1)

Written by hand on 2026-11-27 (rails desk, lane F). Five "are you sure?" cards for forms that now open inside the dock. Until you approve a card, a normal visitor sees a plain line ("<form name>. This runs now, on your account.") and only an admin sees the draft, marked "Draft wording, not approved yet". None has anything in {curly braces}.

## How to answer

Put an x in "approve" if the card text is right, or in "change to" and write the sentence you want.

## 1. Change your photo (upload-profile-photo)

The form shows your pictures; you choose one and press Save photo.

[Public: other people see the change]

> **The card will say:** Use this picture as your profile photo? It shows on your page and in the directory.

**Answer:**  [ ] approve   [ ] change to: ______________________________

## 2. Change your page (profile-section-toggle)

You choose Blog or Store and switch it on or off for your own page.

[Public: other people see the change]

> **The card will say:** Show or hide this part of your own page? Visitors to your page see the change at once.

**Answer:**  [ ] approve   [ ] change to: ______________________________

## 3. Retire a pen name (retire-pen-name)

You pick one of your pen names from a list.

[Public: other people see the change]

> **The card will say:** Retire this pen name? Its page and byline are hidden. You can bring it back later.

**Answer:**  [ ] approve   [ ] change to: ______________________________

## 4. Bring back a pen name (restore-pen-name)

You pick one of your retired pen names from a list.

[Public: other people see the change]

> **The card will say:** Bring this pen name back? Its page and byline show again.

**Answer:**  [ ] approve   [ ] change to: ______________________________

## 5. Post it (post-to-a-site)

You choose where (your own blog, or a feed of an organisation you belong to), write a title and the text. It is published at once.

[Public: other people see the change]

> **The card will say:** Post this now? It goes live there straight away, and people who visit see it.

**Answer:**  [ ] approve   [ ] change to: ______________________________
