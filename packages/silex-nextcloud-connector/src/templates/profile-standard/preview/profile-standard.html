Statement

I paint on salvaged panel — cupboard doors, dropped leaves of tables, whatever arrives with a history already in it. The work is mostly about repair: what a surface looks like once it has been mended and the mend has been left visible.

Alongside the studio I run small groups, six or eight people, where we spend as much time looking as making. People arrive wanting technique and usually leave with attention instead, which I think is the better trade.
