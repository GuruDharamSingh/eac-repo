Most publishing systems treat a post as a row that happens to contain prose. The row has a title, a body, a timestamp; the page renders them in whatever the framework's default stylesheet says. The result is legible and completely anonymous.

The difference between that and something worth reading is not decoration. It is a small number of measurements, held consistently: line length near sixty-five characters, leading generous enough that the paragraph reads as a block of grey, type large enough that nobody leans in.
