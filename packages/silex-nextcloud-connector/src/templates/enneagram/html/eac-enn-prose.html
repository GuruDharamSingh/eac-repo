What is the Enneagram?

The Enneagram is a symbolic map of movement and transformation, rooted in esoteric mathematics, music theory through the law of octaves, the law of three, and the law of seven as taught by G.I. Gurdjieff.

There are 9 points, each representing a general stage or quality which requires a specific change of quality to transform. Each point sits between two adjacent types — its wings — just as a musical scale moves from one tone to the next.

Individuals generally utilize one of three centers of intelligence to process their identity and frame their needs and place in the world. Each of the 9 points represents a relationship to the core need of one of these three Centers of Intelligence: Body, Heart, Head.

Consider it a radar scanner of identity that surfaces the core tensions and struggles you experience — and a guide toward understanding the deeper motivations that shape how you relate, protect, desire, and disconnect.
