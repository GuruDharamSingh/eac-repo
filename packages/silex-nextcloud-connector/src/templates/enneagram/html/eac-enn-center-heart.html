Heart Center

Energy of Image

(Types 2, 3, 4)

Core Need: To establish authentic identity independent of others’ perceptions, balancing relational attunement with genuine self-definition. This center monitors internal experiences and imagery that have value-laden reactions to the impressions of the external and inner world — self-image, preferences, and assessments of others through affect.

  • Inflation of self through giving and relational service (2)
  • Adaptation of image to continuously match the expectations of the environment (3)
  • Intensification of individuality through contrast, longing, and inner depth (4)
