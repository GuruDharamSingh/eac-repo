Gut Center

Energy of the Body

(Types 8, 9, 1)

Core Need: To establish internal stability through sustainable boundaries, congruence between intention and action, psychophysiological freedom, and instinctual alignment with “gut” presence. This center seeks somatic certainty — a grounded sense of “I am,” rooted in orientation to resistance, impact, and bodily knowing.

  • Expansion of boundaries and intensified control (8)
  • Avoidance of conflict and assimilation of disturbances through dissociation (9)
  • Hyper-correction of impulses through systematic adherence to internalized standards (1)
