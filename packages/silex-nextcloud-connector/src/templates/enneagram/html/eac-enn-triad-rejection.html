Object Relations

Rejection (−)

“This need disrupts clarity — I must reduce, control, or go without it.” Identified with constantly releasing extra charge. Deepening into getting the center’s needs met with the most all-encompassing and effective strategies. These types slow down movement that tries to go beyond the center’s need.

  • Body-Rejection (8): Asserting willpower and expanding psychological and physical control — rejecting vulnerability and the gut’s need for tranquil consistency in favor of constant outward movement.
  • Image-Rejection (2): Subverting the heart center’s need for worth by becoming the beacon of love and validation — severing the need to process the complexities of self.
  • Mind-Rejection (5): Withdrawing from the head center’s need to update its frame of reference in favor of deep, narrow understanding — becoming expert at granular observation removed from external obligation.
