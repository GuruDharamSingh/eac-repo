Head Center

Energy of Thought

(Types 5, 6, 7)

Core Need: To arrive at certainty and inner guidance through the intellect, navigating the gap between thought and trust. Driven by increasing capabilities and curiosity, this center discerns, reformulates, and creates perspectives at will — and by nature can sever itself from direct contact with immediate reality as it prioritizes how things move in time.

  • Withdrawal into knowledge as a substitute for direct engagement with the world (5)
  • Scanning for threat and building systems of loyalty to navigate doubt and uncertainty (6)
  • Forward projection into possibility and plans to outrun present anxiety (7)
