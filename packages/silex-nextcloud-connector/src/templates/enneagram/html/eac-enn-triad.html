Object Relations

Attachment (=)

Avoiding destabilization by limiting friction. Driven by a persistent toggling between over-adaptation and withdrawal, designed to preserve access to the source of the need. This strategy suppresses individuated impulses that might jeopardize relational security — identified with preventing surges in charge and questioning the “why” of the center’s need.

  • Body-Attachment (9): Hyper-awareness of the environment creates a dissociation from immediate bodily impulses — a sensitivity to being perturbed through blurred boundaries between self and world.
  • Image-Attachment (3): Hyper-awareness of standards creates a split between the raw personality and the curated, well-performed self that continuously updates to match.
  • Mind-Attachment (6): Hyper-awareness of the duality between truth and falsity creates hypervigilance toward errors of logic and unreliable information.
