Object Relations

Frustration (+)

“Something essential is missing — I should fix, chase, or perfect it.” Ego-identified with continuously increasing charge. Seeking to move beyond the center’s need for something more “right.” Judges and moves away from the center’s need for full perception in favor of what is more ideal, refined, or expansive.

  • Body-Frustration (1): Hyper-correction of the gut’s instinctual impulses through systematic standards — rejecting the fluid quality of being-presence to impose moral and intellectual order.
  • Image-Frustration (4): Amplification of uniqueness and inner depth to mark the gap between self and world — chasing an ideal form of value too abstract to be fully realized externally.
  • Mind-Frustration (7): Forward projection into new possibilities and frames of reference — the head center no longer primarily used as a perceptive tool but as a means of escape from lack of stimulation.
