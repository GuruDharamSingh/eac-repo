What changes from desktop

One column, thumb-side controls

  1. 1Media leads. The desktop's right column becomes a 300px band under the status bar. Tabs stay; photos get dots instead of a counter. Street View, film and 3D still load on tap.
  2. 2The stage scrolls. Same masthead, but the title may wrap and ‹ › are 44px targets. The narrative sits at 1.22rem so it still reads as the voice of the walk.
  3. 3Place in the flow. The one-line place face sits under the narrative instead of growing over it. Tapping it pushes the place surface as a bottom sheet; ← or the scrim goes back.
  4. 4The dock. Strip and foot are fixed at the bottom. No hover on a phone, so the open stop's card widens to show its kind; the strip scrolls it into view.
  5. 5Book as a sheet. The same compact event surface as desktop, full width, with 44px steppers and a 48px Book now.
