2Where a wizard's steps can live in a surface

The surface frame is masthead, body (with an optional rail) and foot, so a wizard can put its steps in any of the three. All four keep one primary action in the foot and let you jump back and forth; none blocks a step on validation. The prototype uses 2a: open Create Tour Wizard.
