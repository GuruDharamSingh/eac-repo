Art-auction on a phone

Design prototype, 2026-11-27. Tap through it on the left. Placeholder pieces and prices; nothing here talks to the market.

What stays a page

  • Lists you browse: the gallery, /lots, a series page, a store front, /center.
  • Each piece and lot keeps its own page (/artworks/[id], /lots/[id]) for shared links and search; on a phone a card opens the same content as a sheet, and the head's ⤢ goes to the page.

What opens as a sheet

  • Piece, lot, artist, and every step of buying, registering, bidding, paying and launching a work.
  • Sheets stack. The crumb ‹ Previous or Esc pops one; ✕ or the dimmed page closes all. The URL follows the top sheet (?surface=artwork:…), as the cms-ui surface system does today.

Sheet rules

  1. Head: kind, crumb, title, and at most one button for the object (Share on a piece or lot, ⤢ its page). Transactions show only the crumb and ✕.
  2. Foot: a status line, then one primary under the thumb, full width, vermilion. A quiet second button only where named (Add to basket, Message).
  3. Heights: piece, lot, delivery and pay rise high; artist sits mid; done sits low.
  4. Names open the artist sheet, everywhere.
  5. Money stays on Stripe's hosted page; the return reopens the done sheet over the page the buyer left.
  6. Kinds carry a colour on the head rule only (artwork, lot, order, pay, done, artist, new work); the primary stays vermilion.

Flows

  • Buy: piece → delivery → pay → Stripe → done.
  • Bid: lot → register (phone, card on file) → lot, registered → bid placed. Outbid: a notice reopens the lot. Won: lot → pay → done.
  • Artist: any name → artist (follow, message, works) → piece.
  • Launch (seller, from /center): photos → details → price and where → preview (the buyer's sheet) → published → share.

Changes from the market today

  • Buy now skips the cart on a phone (the basket stays as the quiet button).
  • Shipping is chosen before paying, inside the delivery sheet; today the quote comes after the order, on /orders/[id].
  • Collect and "arrange with the artist" are offered as choices, not a link.
  • Registration returns to the lot with a "You can bid" notice (?registration=done is not read today).
  • Buy-now on a lot is a sheet, not window.confirm.
  • The artist sheet adds Follow; visitors have no follow today.

For the developer to decide

D3. Interac eTransfer in the pay sheet for every store, or only where the store accepts it?

D5. The market's light paper look, or the handoff's dark shell, for sheets?
