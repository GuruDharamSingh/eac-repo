Box Studies: three sections, three densities

Three hand-composed 900 × 1300 sections that study the boxes themselves, after another pass over the specimens: a dense wall of agate with booking codes, a staggered board of boxes in every size and a few colours, and a section where every box speaks in its own typeface on one steady grid.
