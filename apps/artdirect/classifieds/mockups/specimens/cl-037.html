Reference reproduction. No liners anywhere: every advertisement is a centred stack in which each line is a different face and size (a fat-face woodtype name, an italic tagline, spaced small caps, a tight Scotch Roman paragraph), category labels are small caps with a full stop between hairlines, cuts (a locomotive, a medallion, a carriage) sit above the stacks, timetables run in two columns with dot leaders, and each ad ends with a tiny insertion code at the right (dec10 3m). The columns are uneven, the paper is yellowed and the right edge is torn.
