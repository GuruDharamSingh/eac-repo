Three more sections, three more kinds

A photo sheet of rooms and spaces to let, where every ad is a halftone over a bold lead; an entertainment page of theatre boxes, show-time agate and poster blocks; and a 1970s situations-vacant page made only of large boxed displays. Each is a whole section: one grid, one head device, one box family, one voice of copy, filled to the foot. The copy is written for an imagined river town of studios; nothing is lifted from the specimens except the devices.
