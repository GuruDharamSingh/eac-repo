Broken Columns: four classified sections

Four hand-composed pages, 900 × 1300 each, in the manner of the first mock-ups. In every one the column grid breaks part-way down the page: a band of six agate columns gives way to three wide ones, a notice grid turns into seven lines of personals, a Victorian page runs beside a comic back page with its own gutters.
