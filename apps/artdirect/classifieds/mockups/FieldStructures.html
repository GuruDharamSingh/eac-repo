Field Structures: patterns bigger than a page

Two generated stretches of the continuous section, each about three and a half page-widths wide and two tall (dashed lines mark where pages would have ended). Under them the usual texture: zones of broken columns, each zone in a theme, boxes varying one from the next. Over them, structures that only make sense at the scale of the field: a Love Lines ribbon that runs diagonally for pages, a row of coupons a thousand pixels long, a circular ad with its copy running round the rim, a staircase of tabs, a river of blank paper, a word three pages wide. The ads pack around them.
